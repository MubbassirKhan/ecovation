Frequently asked questions

How do PET acoustic panels improve sound?

PET panels absorb sound energy and reduce reverberation, which improves speech clarity. By placing them on ceilings, walls, or as screens, we lower echo without making the room feel closed. This is especially useful for open offices and meeting rooms where people need to focus and communicate.

Can acoustic panels match our brand colors?

Yes. PET panels are available in many colors and can be cut into custom shapes. This lets you match brand palettes while solving sound issues. For detailed options, see the acoustic panels page.

What is included in a workspace fit-out?

A fit-out typically includes layout planning, material selection, coordination with electrical and lighting needs, and on-site execution. We also integrate acoustic strategies so the space remains comfortable over time. Explore the full scope on the workspaces page.
